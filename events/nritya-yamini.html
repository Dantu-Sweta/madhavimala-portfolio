Yukie Indian Dance Company "Sampatti" · Navya Nataka Samiti

Nritya Yamini (Bandhan)

◆

"Bandhan" — Celebrating the Indo-Japanese Friendship. Yukie Indian Dance Company "Sampatti" (Nagoya, Japan) and Navya Nataka Samiti (Hyderabad, India) presented Nritya Yamini, an evening of Indian classical dances — Odissi & Kuchipudi.

  • Date Tuesday, 3 June 2025 · 6:45–8:45 pm
  • Venue Minato Cultural Theatre, Nagoya, Japan
  • Odissi Yukie Sato (Nagoya)
  • Kuchipudi Madhavi Mala, Reni Sreejith & B.S. Mekhala (Hyderabad, India)

Presented as part of Navya Nataka Samiti's 67 years of nurturing art. Best compliments: FICO, Nagoya & Y's Men's Club of Hyderabad.
